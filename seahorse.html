SEAHORSE FACTS

Name

The Latin name for seahorse is Hippocampus which means "Horse Caterpillar"

What is a Seahorse

Seahorses are fish. They live in water, breathe through gills and have a swim bladder.

Eyesight

Seahorses have excellent eyesight and can move each eye independently.

Snout

Seahorses use their snouts like a vacuum to suck up food.

Diet

Seahorses eat small crustacea such as Mysis Shrimp.

Tail

They use their tail to grip plants and stay stable in water.

Colour

Seahorses can change colour to match their surroundings.

Body

They have bony plates instead of scales.
